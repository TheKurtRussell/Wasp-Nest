🐝 Wasp Nest — M5Stack Tab5

A Wi-Fi / BLE / 802.15.4 recon workbench. This page installs the P4 application onto your Tab5 over USB-C. Authorized testing only.

Step 1 — Flash the P4 app

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  2. Plug the Tab5 into the computer with a USB-C cable.
  3. Click Install and pick the Tab5's serial port.

Step 2 — Give the C6 its firmware (one time)

The radio chip (C6) isn't reachable from the browser — the P4 flashes it for you. Just:

  1. Download merged-c6.bin.
  2. Copy it to the Tab5's microSD card as /waspnest/c6_service.bin.
  3. Insert the card and boot. On the Status tab, tap Flash C6 (or it runs automatically on first boot if the C6 isn't responding).

You only do this once; afterwards you can re-flash just the P4 app from Step 1 whenever you update.
